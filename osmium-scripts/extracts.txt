# The Geofabrik extracts each region is built from: one per line, the region,
# then the extract's path on download.geofabrik.de without the -latest.osm.pbf.
#
# Europe is assembled from country extracts rather than downloaded whole: the
# continent is 32GB+ and the server has less disk than that, while the largest
# country is under 5GB. Each country is downloaded, filtered down to railways
# and deleted before the next one comes in; prepare.sh then merges the filtered
# files. Neighbouring extracts overlap at the border, and the merge keeps one
# copy of every object - the newest, if the two extracts are a day apart.
#
# The list is the countries on the map, plus the ones routes run through
# without stopping: a route that crosses a country left out here loses its
# track and goes invalid on the next import. Liechtenstein carries
# Buchs - Feldkirch, Monaco the coast line from Nice to Menton. A country can
# also be here ahead of the map: Portugal, Hungary, Romania, Bulgaria and
# Croatia are downloaded so their track shows on the admin map, and join
# SUPPORTED_COUNTRIES (src/lib/shared/constants.ts) once routes are defined.
#
# Adding a country: add its line (the name as it appears on Geofabrik's region
# page), commit, push, deploy.
